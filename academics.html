Our Curriculum

Building a Strong Foundation

At Wamboga Memorial, we follow the National Curriculum, enhanced with modern teaching methodologies to ensure our pupils excel. We focus on a holistic approach that balances academics with character development.

Nursery Section (Baby - Top Class)

We provide a warm, play-based learning environment that develops early literacy, numeracy, and social skills. Our trained caregivers ensure a safe and loving atmosphere.

Primary Section (P1 - P7)

Our primary curriculum is rigorous and engaging. We prepare pupils for the Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) while instilling critical thinking, problem-solving, and a lifelong love for learning.
