Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Wamboga Memorial Nursery & Primary School was founded to honor the legacy of a community pillar, dedicated to shaping the future of young minds. Over the years, we have grown into a premier educational institution, providing a safe, nurturing, and disciplined environment for children to thrive.

We believe that education goes beyond the classroom. It is about instilling values, building character, and equipping learners with the practical skills they need to navigate life successfully. Our motto, "Hardwork and Discipline Make Us Success," is not just a saying—it is a way of life here.
