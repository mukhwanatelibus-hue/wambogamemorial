Entry Requirements

  • Nursery (Baby - Top Class): Birth Certificate copy, 2 passport photos.
  • Primary (P1 - P7): Report card from previous school, PLE results (for P5-P7), Birth Certificate, 2 passport photos.
  • Age Requirement: Baby class (3 years), P1 (6 years).
